# Interview Talking Points

## One-sentence project description

I am building a small proof-of-concept that links public TCGA lung-adenocarcinoma H&E whole-slide images to genomic annotations, initially testing patient-level prediction of EGFR mutation status using pretrained pathology embeddings and multiple-instance learning.

## What this demonstrates

- I can design reproducible analyses of paired molecular and clinical datasets.
- I understand that WSI labels are patient or slide level, not patch level.
- I know patient-level splitting is essential to avoid patch-level leakage.
- I am interested in biological interpretation, not just predictive performance.

## Responsible limitations statement

This is an exploratory public-data demonstration. It is not a diagnostic model, does not establish causality, and requires independent external validation before any clinical interpretation.
